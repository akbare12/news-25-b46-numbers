A numbers-led front page for the make.fun news-image bounty
`news-image-20261125`: **50 stories across 10 regions**, led by a
hero trio built on giant figures.

## Hero trio

1. **20%** — Trump hits China with a 20% tariff and duties on
   14 partners. Why it matters: higher import costs will lift US
   prices and redraw Asian supply chains.
2. **-6.16%** — Kospi dives to 4,697.51 as Samsung falls 9.87%
   and SK Hynix 11.05%. Why it matters: the selloff erases
   billions from chip giants and shakes confidence in the AI boom.
3. **1ST** — Shenzhou-24 takes Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali
   to Tiangong, the first foreign astronaut on the station. Why
   it matters: the first foreign guest aboard Tiangong opens
   China's station to the world.

Also inside: the Bogor gold mine collapse (9 dead, 17 trapped),
a BY THE NUMBERS strip, and 47 compact numbered cards, each
tagged C (core), R (regional) or CL (close-up). A two-line DATE
CHECK in the footer confirms Wednesday, 25 November 2026.

The 10 regions covered: North America, Europe, East Asia,
Southeast Asia, South Asia, Middle East, Africa, Latin America,
Oceania and Global.

## How it works

- `gen.py` renders `entry.png` (1024x640) at build time with Pillow.
- `main.py` is standard-library only: it copies `entry.png` into
  `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. Run it with `python3 main.py`.
